# {Person name} — profile (what they know / have done)

Purpose: answer or draft content *as* {Person name} on a topic, informed by
their real background — not just their phrasing.

## Load order

| Step | Read | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `profile.md` | Always — the curated, structured summary |
| 2 | `corpus/*.md` | Only for verbatim wording (exact numbers, titles, dates) |

`profile.md` is derived from `corpus/` the same way a voice bundle's
`categorization.md` is derived from its `corpus/posts.md` — corpus is raw and
untouched, `profile.md` is the curated read.
