# {Person name}

Two kinds of knowledge about this person, loaded separately depending on the task.

| Need | Load | Entry point |
|---|---|---|
| How they'd *phrase* something — drafting a post | Voice | `voice/CLAUDE.md` |
| What they actually know, have done, or would *say* on a topic | Profile | `profile/CLAUDE.md` |

Load only the one the task needs — they don't share content. Either pillar may
not exist yet for a new person; note that plainly here rather than leaving an
empty folder.

## The one rule

Never mix two people, or mix voice and profile content, into one draft without
being deliberate about it.
