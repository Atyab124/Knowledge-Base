# Voice: Networking & Relationship Capital

Posts: 12a ("Yesterday taught me..." / Hub71 event recap), 12b ("I Think I Finally Get It." / "People are the most valuable currency you own").

Read [../dos-and-donts.md](../dos-and-donts.md) first — this file only covers what's specific to this category.

## What this category is for

Gratitude and access, stated plainly — credibility by association, but only ever earned association. The underlying claim across both posts is the same: who you spend time with changes your output. One version tells it through a specific event, the other as pure reflection.

## Shape

1. **State the takeaway before telling the story** — the reverse of Sharing Knowledge, which builds to the lesson: *"Yesterday taught me that you grow fastest when you walk into rooms you don't know yet."* / *"I Think I Finally Get It. People are the most valuable currency you own."*
2. **12a (event-recap variant): chronological, one beat per person.** Every name gets a full name, a one-line credential or title, and the single specific thing they said or shared — never a vague "great meeting people":
   > Then on stage came Karim Davis Dib, the most goated mentor in the world.
   > He spoke about how founders should be a problem looking for a solution, not the other way around.

   Direct quotes are used when they're strong enough to stand alone: *"Adaptive learning and good judgment at high velocity — that's the most important skill for founders today."*
3. **12b (reflection variant): no event, no names, heavy short-clause repetition for emphasis** instead of anecdote:
   > It's strange, because nothing else changes.
   > Same work ethic. Same ambitions. Just better company.
4. **Gratitude stated specifically, never generically**: *"I'm genuinely grateful for the few who've done that for me lately. They're rare."*
5. **Close warm and understated, not on a hard aphorism** — this is the one category that doesn't end on a quotable punchline the way Hot Takes and Promoting My Work do: *"Not bad for a Tuesday afternoon."* / *"they make all the difference. And I hope I can do the same for them."*

## Register

The cleanest grammar and punctuation in the whole file — full apostrophes, complete sentences, more literary word choice than any other category (*"Walked in alone. Walked out with ideas, mentors, and friends."*). This is not the place for dropped apostrophes or lowercase sentence starts.

## Checklist

- Does every name carry a real credential or a specific thing they said — never a bare name-drop?
- Is gratitude specific (what they actually did) rather than generic ("thanks to everyone")?
- Does the close stay warm/understated rather than reaching for a hard, quotable aphorism?
- Is the grammar clean throughout — no dropped apostrophes, no fragment-heavy lines?
