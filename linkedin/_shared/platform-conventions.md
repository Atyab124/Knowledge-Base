# LinkedIn platform conventions

Facts confirmed true across every person's corpus in `people/` so far — not
personal voice, not derived per-person. Load this alongside a person's own
`dos-and-donts.md`, always.

**When to add a fact here**: only after it's independently confirmed in at
least 2 people's actual corpora (grep the text, don't assume). One person
doing something is their voice. Two people independently doing the same
mechanical thing, unprompted, is a platform convention. If a fact has a
carve-out for one person (e.g. one voice permits hashtags on paid posts,
another never uses them at all), it's not a shared fact — it stays in that
person's own file even if it looks similar on the surface.

## Never use em-dashes

Zero appear in either person's own authored text (14-post and 11-post
corpora checked). Split into two sentences instead.

## No exclamation marks

Zero appear in either corpus, regardless of how enthusiastic or urgent the
content is. Enthusiasm and urgency are carried by pacing, stakes, and
sentence length — never `!`.

## LinkedIn's composer doesn't render markdown

Bold, italics, and other markdown syntax pasted into a post render as literal
asterisks/underscores, not formatting. Whitespace and line breaks carry the
emphasis markdown can't — never `**bold**`.

## One thought per line, heavy blank space between them

Both corpora format this way regardless of how different their content or
register is — most paragraphs are 1–2 sentences, many are sentence fragments
alone on their own line. This is the shared baseline rhythm; a person's own
voice file may add more on top of it (length, exact register) but neither
corpus violates it.
