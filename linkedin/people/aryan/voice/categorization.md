# Aryan — Post Categories

Source: [`posts.md`](corpus/posts.md) — 11 posts.

Five categories, derived fresh from what's actually here — none copied from
Atyab's set (categories are per-person, per the method). The split skews hard
toward promotion: 10 of 11 posts end in an explicit call to action, only 1 is
pure commentary. That's the single biggest voice difference from Atyab, whose
set has zero engagement-bait CTAs.

## The five categories

| # | Category | Posts | What it's for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Webinar Pitch / Case-for-Attending** | 1, 3, 4 | Build the argument for why the reader needs this specific webinar |
| 2 | **Webinar Social Proof / Momentum** | 5, 6, 9 | Prove demand is real — counts, DMs, testimonial screenshots |
| 3 | **System Breakdown / Lead Magnet** | 7, 8, 11 | Numbered teardown of a proprietary system, real stats, free-resource CTA |
| 4 | **Product Case Study** | 2 | Narrative case study for a specific paid product (Viktor), paid-media tagged |
| 5 | **Technical Opinion / Hot Take** | 10 | Pure commentary — no product, no CTA |

Voice guide for each: [tone/webinar-pitch.md](tone/webinar-pitch.md) · [tone/webinar-social-proof.md](tone/webinar-social-proof.md) · [tone/system-breakdown.md](tone/system-breakdown.md) · [tone/product-case-study.md](tone/product-case-study.md) · [tone/technical-opinion.md](tone/technical-opinion.md)

## Post-by-post

1. **"For most engineers, every advancement in AI means less job stability."** → **Webinar Pitch**. Reframes an industry-news anxiety (AI progress) as an opportunity specifically for the reader, via a "your employer sees you as replaceable, an SMB sees you as essential" argument.
2. **"I hate AI that gives me homework."** → **Product Case Study**. A specific paid product (Viktor), told as a 4-day narrative with timestamps, ends with hashtags and a hard CTA — the only post in the set tagged `#PaidMedia`.
3. **"If you've been paying attention, you know that OpenAI just released GPT-6 Astra..."** → **Webinar Pitch**. Same underlying argument as #1, hung on a fresh news hook (a model release) for urgency.
4. **"People have been asking why Wednesday's webinar is for technical employees only."** → **Webinar Pitch**. FAQ/objection-handling framing — exclusivity as the pitch.
5. **"After just 24 hours I already have nearly 300 engineers who are signed up..."** → **Webinar Social Proof**. Short, registration-count flex, same webinar as 1/3/4.
6. **"GM. I've gotten over 100 DMs in the past 2 weeks..."** → **Webinar Social Proof**. DM-volume flex, same webinar.
7. **"I've made $100,000+ connecting creators with brands."** → **System Breakdown**. Six-step numbered system, real stats, "Free access below."
8. **"I built a $100K LinkedIn writer from 11 Claude skills."** → **System Breakdown**. Eleven-skill breakdown, real stats (30M views, $1M+ business), "Free access below."
9. **"DM from a former engineer who does Fractional AI Consulting, basically still retired."** → **Webinar Social Proof**. Testimonial screenshot + an interest-check poll for a *future* webinar, not yet scheduled.
10. **"I hate building frontends with AI."** → **Technical Opinion**. The one post with zero promotional payload — no product, no link, no CTA. Structurally closest to Atyab's voice of anything in this set.
11. **"I just open-sourced an entire marketing department."** → **System Breakdown**. Same shape as 7/8 — named system, folder-tree visual instead of a numbered list, ends in a download link.

## Judgment calls worth flagging

- **Categories 4 and 5 have one example each** — same caveat as Atyab's Behind-the-Scenes: treat their voice docs as first drafts until more posts confirm the pattern.
- **1 and 2 are both "webinar promotion" at root.** Split into two categories because the internal shape differs enough to need separate tone docs (long argumentative essay vs. short proof-flash), not because the underlying purpose differs. If that distinction doesn't feel load-bearing to you, they could merge.
