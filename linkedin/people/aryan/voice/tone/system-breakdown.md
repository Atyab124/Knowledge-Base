# Voice: System Breakdown / Lead Magnet

Posts: 7 ("I've made $100,000+ connecting creators with brands"), 8 ("I built a $100K LinkedIn writer from 11 Claude skills"), 11 ("I just open-sourced an entire marketing department").

Read [../dos-and-donts.md](../dos-and-donts.md) first — this file only covers what's specific to this category.

## What this category is for

The most information-dense category — teardown of a proprietary system he
built, real stats as proof it works, then a free (not paid, not a webinar)
resource as the CTA. This is the closest thing to "sharing knowledge" in his
set, but it's still built to convert, not just to teach.

## Shape

1. **Open on the dollar/view/result headline, stated as fact, no hedging**: *"I've made $100,000+ connecting creators with brands."* / *"I built a $100K LinkedIn writer from 11 Claude skills. It generated 30M views and built my $1M+ AI business."*
2. **One line naming the old, worse way of doing this** before the system: *"Claude never sees the experiences that make them credible... So it guesses."* / *"A talent manager takes 20% of every deal to do this by hand. Now you keep the 20%."*
3. **Numbered system, one line of concrete payoff per component, named like a role or a tool**: *"1) Sponsor Radar: every brand that paid a creator in your niche in the last 90 days..."* Post 11 varies this with a folder-tree diagram instead of a numbered list — same job, different visual device.
4. **→ arrow-prefixed proof stats immediately after the breakdown**, compressed to one line each: *"→ 15,811 brands scored. 600 contacted. 168 replies. 28% reply rate."*
5. **Close with a free-access CTA**, never a paid one: "Free access below," "here is the skill to download," "Comment X to get access."

## Register

Highest number-density of any category — real, exact figures throughout ($100,000+, 15,811, 217 of 531, $40,000,000,000, 10M+, 4.5B+). Numbered lists and arrows do more work here than sentences. Still zero exclamation marks, zero hashtags.

## Checklist

- Does the opening headline state a real result as fact, no "I think" or "maybe"?
- Is the system broken into named, numbered (or diagrammed) components, each with one concrete payoff line — not paragraphs of explanation?
- Are the proof stats real, exact, and placed right after the breakdown, not scattered throughout?
- Is the CTA framed as free/immediate access, not a paid product or a future webinar — that's what separates this from [product-case-study.md](product-case-study.md) and [webinar-pitch.md](webinar-pitch.md)?
