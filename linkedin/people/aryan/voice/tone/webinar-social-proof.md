# Voice: Webinar Social Proof / Momentum

Posts: 5 ("After just 24 hours I already have nearly 300 engineers..."), 6 ("GM. I've gotten over 100 DMs in the past 2 weeks..."), 9 ("DM from a former engineer who does Fractional AI Consulting...").

Read [../dos-and-donts.md](../dos-and-donts.md) first — this file only covers what's specific to this category.

## What this category is for

Short, fast proof-of-demand updates for the same webinar/offer [webinar-pitch.md](webinar-pitch.md)
already argued for. The argument is done; this category's only job is to show
momentum is real, using a count or a screenshot, not more reasoning.

## Shape

1. **Lead with one concrete proof number or artifact in the first line** — a headcount, a DM count, or a testimonial screenshot: *"After just 24 hours I already have nearly 300 engineers who are signed up..."* / *"I've gotten over 100 DMs in the past 2 weeks..."*
2. **One line restating the stakes, much shorter than a full pitch post** — a compressed reminder, not a re-argument: *"This is not something you wanna miss if you're technical..."*
3. **Close fast with the CTA** — register, sign up, or (post 9) a soft interest-check poll for a webinar not yet scheduled: *"let me know in the comments if you'd attend."*

## Register

The shortest posts in the whole set — a third the length of a [webinar-pitch.md](webinar-pitch.md) post. No rhetorical questions, no reader-fear framing — the work of persuasion already happened elsewhere; this is pure momentum signal. Post 9 leans on an attached DM screenshot as the proof itself rather than a stated number — treat a real screenshot as equally valid evidence to a real count.

## Checklist

- Is the proof point a real, specific number or a real artifact (screenshot), not a vague "lots of interest"?
- Is the post short — a flash, not an essay? If it's building a case, it belongs in [webinar-pitch.md](webinar-pitch.md) instead.
- Does it still end in a CTA, even when short?
