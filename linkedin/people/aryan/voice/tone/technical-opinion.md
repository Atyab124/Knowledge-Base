# Voice: Technical Opinion / Hot Take

Post: 10 ("I hate building frontends with AI") — the only clear example in the source so far.

Read [../dos-and-donts.md](../dos-and-donts.md) first — this file only covers what's specific to this category.

> **First draft, not a settled pattern** — same caveat as Atyab's Behind-the-Scenes and this person's own Product Case Study file. One example only.

## What this category is for

The single exception to this entire voice: a post with zero promotional
payload — no product, no webinar, no link, no CTA. Pure technical observation.
This is structurally the closest thing to Atyab's voice anywhere in Aryan's
corpus — hook, observation, aphoristic close, nothing sold.

## Shape

1. **Open on a personal-pain declarative hook**, same device as [product-case-study.md](product-case-study.md)'s opener but with nothing attached to it afterward: *"I hate building frontends with AI."*
2. **Name specific tools and specific failure modes**, concretely, not vaguely: *"Claude, Codex, and Kimi can generate a dashboard in 30 seconds. Then I spend the next six hours explaining that the main button is invisible, the table breaks on mobile..."*
3. **State what was already tried**, to preempt the obvious rebuttal: *"I've installed the design skills. I've fed it screenshots from Linear and Stripe."*
4. **Land the real claim in one plain sentence**: *"AI is incredible at writing frontend code. It is still terrible at knowing when a product feels cheap, confusing, or annoying to use."*
5. **Close on a compressed, quotable aphorism** — no CTA, no link, nothing sold: *"The first 70% takes 30 seconds. The last 30% ruins your Saturday."*

## Register

Shortest paragraphs of any category, same clean grammar as everywhere else in this voice. The absence of a CTA is the entire point — don't add one; that would collapse this into [system-breakdown.md](system-breakdown.md) or [product-case-study.md](product-case-study.md).

## Checklist

- Is there genuinely no product, link, or ask anywhere in the post?
- Does it name real, specific tools and failure modes rather than a generic "AI struggles with design"?
- Does it close on a short, quotable line that reframes the opening claim, not a summary?
